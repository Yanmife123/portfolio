## Quick Reference — Role Definitions (applies to all combos)

- **Primary color** — your identity/action color. Used *sparingly* (buttons, links, active states) so it stands out.
- **Secondary color** — the "canvas" color. Carries most of the visual weight (backgrounds, section fills).
- **Dark Neutral** — anchors text and dark-mode; not pure black, usually tinted toward the palette.
- **Light Neutral** — usually white or near-white; used for cards/text-on-dark to create layering and contrast.
- **Accent (optional)** — a tint/shade of primary for tags, badges, or secondary CTAs.
